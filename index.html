Notation Quick Guide

Infix (A + B): Operator sits between operands. Requires parentheses and precedence rules to remove ambiguity.
Postfix / RPN (A B +): Operator follows operands. Evaluated left-to-right with a single stack; no parentheses needed.
Prefix / Polish (+ A B): Operator precedes operands. Evaluated right-to-left (or recursively); no parentheses needed.
